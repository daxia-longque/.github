We specialize in the research, development, and production of advanced communication modules. Our cumulative shipments have exceeded hundreds of millions of units worldwide.

- **Bluetooth / BLE:** Low energy consumption, high stability.
- **4G / LTE:** CAT1/CAT4 modules for wide-area coverage.
- **LoRa / LPWAN:** Long-range, low-power IoT solutions.
- **Wi-Fi:** High-speed data transmission modules.
- **GNSS / GPS:** High-precision positioning technology.
